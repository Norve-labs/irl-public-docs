# Free & Open

IRL is free to use. There are no per-agent fees and no paid tiers today.

| What | Licence | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [IRL Engine](https://github.com/macropulse-lab/irl) | FSL-1.1-ALv2: any use except offering IRL to others as a competing commercial product or service; each release becomes Apache 2.0 two years after publication | Free |
| [IRL Gateway](https://github.com/macropulse-lab/irl-gateway) (MCP) | MIT | Free |
| [Python](https://github.com/macropulse-lab/irl-sdk-python) and [TypeScript](https://github.com/macropulse-lab/irl-sdk-ts) SDKs | MIT | Free |
| [irl-verify](https://github.com/macropulse-lab/irl-verify) | MIT | Free, for everyone, forever |
| Public sandbox at [irl.macropulse.live](https://irl.macropulse.live) | — | Free, best effort |

## What the layers mean now

L1 (audit rail) and L2 (signed market truth) are capabilities of the engine,
not price tiers: both are free. L3 (TEE / ZK, prove without revealing) is
research. See the [whitepaper](whitepaper.md), §2.2.

## Commercial use

Using IRL inside your own firm, including for live trading, is allowed under
the FSL. Hosting IRL for others, embedding it in a product you sell, or
white-labelling it needs a commercial licence: hello@macropulse.live.

## Later

A hosted offering for teams (managed IRL, retention guarantees, compliance
